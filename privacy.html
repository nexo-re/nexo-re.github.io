Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026. In plain words: we collect only what we need to reply to you and to understand how the site is used.

Who we are

This website is run by Nexoré (“we”, “us”), a web design studio. You can reach us at nexoree@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Email and Instagram: if you contact us there, we see what you send and your email address or Instagram account name. You choose what to include.
  • Site usage (analytics): if Google Analytics is switched on, anonymised information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type. We ask Google to anonymise IP addresses, and we do not measure visitors whose browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, send a quote and, if you hire us, deliver the work.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.

We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing emails you did not ask for.

Who processes it for us

  • Google Analytics provides site statistics (only when enabled).
  • Instagram (Meta) and your email provider handle messages you send through them, under their own policies.

Fonts and images on this site are hosted by us, not by a third party.

Cookies

The site itself does not set cookies. If Google Analytics is switched on, Google may set cookies to tell visits apart. You can block them in your browser settings or by sending a “Do Not Track” signal.

How long we keep it

We keep emails and messages for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any project that follows, then delete them. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention setting.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email nexoree@gmail.com and we will reply within 24 hours.

Children

This site is for businesses and adults, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
