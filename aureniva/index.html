AURÉNIVA INTERIORS is a demo interior-design website by Nexoré. It opens with a ten-second film of a bare room becoming a finished home that plays as you scroll, then lists eight kinds of work (modular kitchens, bedrooms and wardrobes, false ceilings and lighting, bathrooms and glass, wall panelling, aluminium doors and windows, offices and shops, custom storage), shows seven real site photographs, explains the five-step process from site visit to handover, answers common questions, and ends with a two-minute brief that is copied and sent by Instagram Direct.
