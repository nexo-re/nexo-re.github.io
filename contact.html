FAQ

Five questions we hear most

How long does a website take?

Most sites go from first call to live in under 7 days. A larger store or one with custom features can take longer, and we confirm the timeline in your quote before you commit.

How much does it cost?

We offer introductory launch pricing for our first clients. The price depends on the number of pages and the features you need, so message us and we will send a clear quote with no obligation.

Do you use templates?

No. Every site is 100% customised around your brand, so it does not look like anyone else’s.

Will my site work on phones and show up on Google?

Yes to both. Sites are built mobile-first and load fast, and they ship with clean structure, unique page titles, descriptions and share images so search engines and social apps understand them.

What happens after I message you?

We reply within 24 hours. We ask a few questions about your business and goals, then send a quote and timeline. To get started you only need your business name and what you sell; logo, photos and text are helpful, and we can guide you on the rest.
