Case studies

Luxury fragrance store · Animation meets real-world

VÉYRA After Dark

The brief
Show how a premium e-commerce store can open like a film and still end in a shop that is easy to buy from.
What we built
A full-screen hero film where the bottle’s story plays, then the bottle lands straight into the product grid. Below it sits a working shop with scent-family filters, a cart drawer and a free-shipping meter. It is designed for landscape screens, so phones are asked to rotate first.
Why it helps a business
  • Scent notes on every card help people choose a fragrance they cannot smell online.
  • The cart shows how close the shopper is to free shipping, which nudges larger orders.
  • The film hands over to the shop without a page change, so the mood is never broken.

Cinematic scroll · Pure animation

Lamborghini Revuelto

The brief
Tell the story of a product’s design, hybrid powertrain and aerodynamics in one continuous piece, without a wall of specifications.
What we built
A full-screen cinematic film with timed captions that starts by itself a second after the page opens. The film is preloaded in the background so playback stays smooth even on mid-range phones. It is built for landscape and shows a rotate prompt on phones held upright.
Why it helps a business
  • A single uninterrupted story holds attention far longer than a spec table.
  • Smart preloading avoids the stutter that makes people leave heavy sites.
  • Hands-free playback means the visitor just watches; there is nothing to figure out.

Jewellery e-commerce · Perfectly balanced

Élvaré

The brief
Let customers buy in the app they already use all day, instead of forcing them through a heavy checkout.
What we built
A jewellery storefront for crystal and pearl pieces with a bag that sends the whole order to WhatsApp, pre-filled. A gift-combo enquiry turns “what should I gift?” into a chat, and one short form collects name, pincode and address so there are fewer follow-up messages.
Why it helps a business
  • Ordering by WhatsApp removes sign-ups and payment pages that cost small shops sales.
  • The gift-combo enquiry starts a conversation that can end in a bigger order.
  • Delivery details are collected up front, so the shop spends less time chasing information.

Streetwear store · 3D-based

Vanta District

The brief
Let shoppers inspect clothing from every side, the way they would in a physical shop.
What we built
A 3D streetwear store where each garment can be spun and zoomed. A size must be chosen before an item goes in the bag, and each colourway shows on the real model. Because it is 3D-heavy, the portfolio warns visitors on older devices before opening it.
Why it helps a business
  • Spinning a garment in 3D helps shoppers pick correctly, which should mean fewer wrong-size returns.
  • Requiring a size first prevents the most common ordering mistake.
  • Showing each colour on the model is more honest than a flat photo.
