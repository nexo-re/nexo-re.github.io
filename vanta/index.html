VANTA DISTRICT

VANTA DISTRICT is a demo 3D streetwear store designed and built by Nexoré. It presents an Autumn/Winter 2026 collection of premium technical apparel made for the nocturnal city: outerwear, hoodies, cargo pants, tees and accessories. Every garment is real 3D geometry drawn live by your device's graphics chip, so shoppers can spin a piece around and inspect it from every side before they buy.

A live colour switcher repaints the 3D model itself rather than swapping a flat photo, and a size must be chosen before an item can be added to the bag, which cuts sizing mistakes and refunds. The store also includes a showroom view, a campaign section, a brand story and a slide-out cart. Because the page uses 3D graphics it runs best on a desktop or a recent phone. It is one of four live demo sites in the Nexoré portfolio, built to show how a fast, 3D-driven online store can hold attention and turn browsing into orders.

The Autumn/Winter 2026 range has five pieces: the NULL SHELL outer shell, the AFTERHOURS 01 hoodie, the GHOSTLINE CARGO trousers, the SIGNAL 06 tee and the DISTRICT-9 accessory. Each comes in two colours, for example Obsidian Black or Concrete Grey for the shell, and Bone White or Faded Black for the tee. Sizes run from S to XL, waist sizes 28 to 36 for the cargo trousers, and one size for the accessory.
