Loopelle - handmade crochet plushies

Loopelle is a handmade crochet plushie shop. This demo site by Nexoré opens with a 3D plushie that is stitched row by row, then turns and glides into the shop. Visitors can browse eight amigurumi friends, filter by category, search, add to a bag, watch each plushie being crocheted, and receive a free e-adoption certificate with their order. Orders and questions are sent by email, so there is no payment page to build.
