Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Loopelle sells handmade crochet plushies. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: for an order, your name, email, phone number (optional), delivery address, order notes and the items in your bag; for the contact form, your name, email, phone number (optional), topic and message.

How the forms work

This website has no database. When you press the button on the checkout or contact form, the website writes an email containing what you entered and opens it as a draft in your own email app (or Gmail). Nothing reaches us until you press Send in that email. We use the details only to process and deliver your order and to answer your message.

What stays in your browser

  • The contents of your shopping bag, so it is still there when you come back.
  • The name you type on a plushie’s adoption certificate.

This data never leaves your device through this website. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Other services this page loads

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) serves our typefaces, so your IP address is shared with Google when the page loads.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) serves the 3D library used for the animated hero.
  • Links to Instagram or WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep emails

Order and message emails are kept for as long as needed to fulfil the order, handle any problem with it, and meet legal or tax obligations. After that they are deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing nexoree@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change how the website handles information, we will update this page and its date.
