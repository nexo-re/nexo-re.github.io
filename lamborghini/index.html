Lamborghini Revuelto: The New Era of V12 Performance

This is an interactive cinematic scroll experience about the Lamborghini Revuelto, built by Nexoré as a demo of film-driven web design. The whole page is a single film: the page scrolls itself and the film follows, frame by frame, while short captions appear over the car at the right moments.

The story covers the Revuelto's design and engineering. It pairs a naturally aspirated 6.5-litre V12 with three electric motors and an electric transverse e-gearbox, for a combined 1,015 CV (1,001 hp) and a 0 to 100 km/h sprint of about 2.5 seconds. Along the way you see the side air intakes, active rear aerodynamics and laminar airflow, the Y-shaped LED headlights and taillights, and the carbon ceramic brakes. Smart preloading keeps the film smooth on mid-range phones, and on a phone the page asks you to turn the device sideways for the best view. It is one of four live demo sites in the Nexoré portfolio.

The film is about fifty seconds long and tells the story through short captions such as Form Meets Function, V12 Engine, V12 Hybrid Power, Aerodynamic Precision, Pure Performance and Beyond the Ordinary. The page starts playing by itself about a second after it opens, so the whole story runs hands-free from the first frame to the last without any scrolling by the visitor.
